# Pacing — minor buildings, upgrades, infantry

Branch `improve/pacing` (from master `ca856c0`). The manager's report: some minor buildings and some
upgrades take too long to build, and infantry is quite slow. Every number below is what the game
computes (`src/data/tuning.js`, `src/sim/production.js` `itemSeconds`) at Normal speed, full power,
one factory; the sidebar shows the same seconds.

## Rules

- **Structures** (`STRUCTURE_BUILD` in tuning.js): the original `buildTime` steps are kept, and so is
  their order. Below 120 steps (the Hi-Tech Factory, Starport and House of IX tier) a structure's time
  shrinks in proportion to its size: `steps × 0.45 × steps / 120`, so time grows with the square of
  the original number; nothing builds in under 2 s. The late-game buildings keep the original pace.
  Before, one wall segment took 18 s and a 5-credit slab 7 s; C&C-era players expect cheap items to be
  near-instant and a power plant in about ten seconds.
- **Upgrades** (`UPGRADE_SECONDS`): 5 s, from 9 s. The original upgrade is 20 steps, between a slab (16)
  and a wall (40); 5 s keeps it there. More important, an upgrade now **starts at once**: before it
  waited behind the unit in hand (up to 47 s behind a Sonic Tank) and the yard refused it while a
  structure was being built. Now the item in hand steps aside, keeping its progress and what it paid,
  and resumes when the upgrade is done. Only a structure waiting to be placed keeps the yard busy.
  Cancelling the set-aside item (right click on its icon, where it shows as queued) refunds it.
- **Infantry** (`SPEED_BASE` in tuning.js): ground speed is `(base + factor / 24) × terrain / 192`;
  the base for foot soldiers (Light Infantry, squads, Troopers, Fremen, Saboteur) is now 0.6 instead
  of the vehicles' 0.25. That lifts the slowest the most (the squad +76 %, the Saboteur +17 %), keeps
  the original order among foot units, and keeps a Combat Tank faster than every foot soldier but the
  Saboteur on any ground they share (a base above 0.66 would break that).

## Side effects checked

- Crushing, capture (infantry walk in faster, the capture rule itself is unchanged), walls and the
  Saboteur, Fremen, formation spreading and pathing: their tests pass unchanged.
- The AI's first attack and wave timers are in seconds and unchanged; waves are bought from the same
  credits. The harvest soak (ten game minutes on four seeds) passes.
- The repair bay still repairs a unit at its build rate (`buildSeconds`): unit build times did not change.
- A build now spends its cost faster (the same price over less time), so with little money a
  build stalls sooner and holds at most one tick's share (under 2 credits) unspent.
- Low power still slows every line down to a quarter (`lineSpeed`). This multiplies every time above
  and is the most likely reason a build still feels slow in play.

## Tests

`tests/pacing.test.mjs` pins the rule: minor buildings (Wind Trap and Silo under 10 s, wall ≤ 6 s,
Gun Turret under 16 s, Rocket Turret under 36 s, a slab 2–3 s), the original order for every pair of
structures, the late-game buildings at the original pace, upgrades at 5 s, the upgrade that starts at
once and resumes the set-aside tank or structure, its refund, and infantry over ten tiles of sand
(squad under 23 s, soldier under 20 s, Trooper Squad under 18.5 s, Heavy Trooper under 15 s, the tank
still first). Tests that pinned the old numbers were updated: the Wind Trap's 21.6 s, the upgrade's
nine seconds, the upgrade that waited behind the tank in hand, and the sidebar's seconds.
