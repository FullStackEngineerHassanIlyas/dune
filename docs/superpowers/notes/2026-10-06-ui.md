# UI round: phone crests, the server's types, allied computers, the original Mentat's size

Branch `feat/allies-crests-mentat-fill` (from `c1a558c`). Four jobs; the graphics job was left out at the manager's
request ("graphics wala point chor kr baki krdo"), and the music remake went to the other agent (src/audio/**).
Each problem was reproduced first; each fix has a test that failed before it, and the visible ones were checked on
the real GPU (headless Chrome, ANGLE/GL) — the Mentat with the player's own PAKs (`original/dune2/`).

## 1. House crests on a phone

- **Before:** at 390 px each house card (~120 px) showed the framed crest, the gold frame taking half of it
  (`left-crest-before-390.png`).
- **Now:** `crestSvg(house)` (src/ui/campaign/crests.js) gives the framed crest (`.cp-crest-wide`) and its bare
  shield (`.cp-crest-narrow`, the baked `*-shield.webp`) one after the other; campaign.css shows the shield instead
  at 620 px and below, in a 4:5 box (`.cp-crest:has(.cp-crest-narrow)`), as art-crests planned. The original
  emblem (`.cpo-emblem`, from the player's files) is untouched. `crestSvg(house, { narrow: false })` gives the framed
  crest alone; `{ variant: 'shield' }` the shield alone, as before. index.js did not need to change.
- **Checked:** 390x844 (shields, charges about twice the size) and 1366x768 (framed, as before).
- **Tests:** tests/crests.test.mjs (the pair and the CSS swap at 620 px), tests/crests-baked.test.mjs (both looks
  baked).

## 2. The server's types

serve.mjs sent WebP (the crests, portraits, results art) as `application/octet-stream`. Now `.webp`, `.gif`,
`.jpeg`, `.avif`, `.ico`, `.oga`, `.opus`, `.flac`, `.m4a`, `.weba`, `.zip` (a player's `original/sega-music.zip`),
`.woff`, `.ttf` and `.txt` have their types too, looked up in any letter case (`contentType()`); a `.PAK` stays
plain bytes. tests/serve.test.mjs: every extension the game ships under assets/, src/ and vendor/ has a type, and
a crest is served as `image/webp`.

## 3. Skirmish: Computers allied

- Set-up row **Computers allied: Off / On** under the opponents (default Off: the free-for-all, as before). On, the
  note says so, and the opponents' note reads "The computers fight as one side"; with one opponent it adds "It
  takes two opponents or more" (nothing to ally).
- Remembered with the other skirmish choices (`dune2-3d.skirmish`, `cleanSetup` keeps `allied: true` only); the
  battle URL carries `allied=1` (Off leaves the URL as it was, so older links and tests are unchanged);

## 4. The original Mentat fills his frame

- **Before:** `figureSizeCss` drew him at a whole number of pixels a pixel wherever one fitted within half a step,
  so he stood smaller than his frame: measured with the real PAKs, Cyril at 1366x768 86 % of the width he could
  take (336 of 390 px), Radnor at 1920x1080 90 %, at 1600x900 95 %, Ammon at 1920x1080 95 % (in principle up to a
  third smaller: a box 1.49 times his size drew him at 1x).
- **Now:** his pictures are enlarged 8x once, when they are made, by Scale2x three times (new
  src/ui/campaign/pixel-scale.js: the EPX/AdvMAME2x rule, no colour ever mixed, staircases become smooth diagonals,
  flat ground, straight edges and lone pixels stay), and the figure fills the portrait at every size, drawn down
  smoothly from the large picture (`image-rendering: auto`; the whole-number container queries are gone). The eye,
  mouth and book frames are enlarged *in place*: drawn over the room, enlarged with two pixels of room around them,
  cut to a box and see-through wherever they leave the enlarged head as it was. The eyes and the book are cut two
  pixels wider than their frame on each side (all a frame changes: Scale2x spreads a change under two pixels), so
  laid over the head they are exactly the whole picture enlarged with that frame in it; the mouth is cut to its own
  box, the one the face rig draws it in, so it is exact inside that box and the few pixels just outside keep the
  head at rest (16 pixels of the 8x picture for Cyril's mouth 2 and 4 and Ammon's mouth 3, about two screen
  pixels at 1366). The rig's mouth box and each eye's lid box are unchanged; the shut eyes' picture and the CSS
  eye and book frames sit in their wider boxes (see Review fixes).
- **Speed:** the scaler runs on the figure's colour indices (one palette for the room and every frame, see-through
  index 0) and the PNGs are written straight from them (new `encodeIndexedPng` in src/formats/png.js), so no pixel
  of the large pictures has its colour looked up. In the browser, making all three Mentats and the emblems takes
  about 0.5 s (was 0.18 s), the longest main-thread block 130–320 ms (was ~70 ms); the campaign's words wait for
  them 1.5 s at most (PICTURES_WAIT). Each figure is ~140 KB of markup (was ~36 KB). 4x would cost a quarter but
  blows up on HiDPI screens; 8x is drawn down on nearly every screen.
- **Checked on the GPU with the real PAKs**, all three houses, at 1920x1080, 1600x900, 1366x768 and 390x844, while
  he speaks: the figure fills its frame everywhere (`fill` 1.000 in all twelve; frame 504x600, 480x600, 456x543 …
  130x155), the head picture 1344x1600 / 1152x1440 / 1216x1520, the mouth moves with the voice (mouth area differs
  frame to frame in an 8-shot series at 90 ms), no seam around the mouth box, no console errors. Shots:
  `left-mentat-after-<size>-<house>-<n>.png`, `talk-1366-atreides-mouths.png`, `cmp-1920-harkonnen.png` (before
  left, after right) in the session scratchpad.
- **Tests:** tests/pixel-scale.test.mjs — Scale2x rounds a staircase off and keeps flat ground, straight edges and
  a lone pixel; the fast form matches the reference rule pixel for pixel, 4x and 8x are two and three passes, no
  colour is new; see-through pixels of any RGB are one colour; the indexed PNG comes back exactly; every frame
  enlarged in place over the head equals the whole picture enlarged with it everywhere when cut two pixels wider,
  inside its box when not. tests/original-pictures.test.mjs: the figure fills its box with no whole-number steps,
  the head and the frames' pictures are 8x their boxes; the rig's mouth and lid boxes are unchanged (mentat-face
  rig tests pass).

## Review fixes

Four minor findings; each reproduced, fixed and checked again.

- **The 8x build was slow on a phone-class CPU.** Measured with the real PAKs in headless Chrome at 4x CPU
  throttling, making all the pictures took 1.7 s, past the 1.5 s the words wait (PICTURES_WAIT), with a 360 ms
  main-thread block. Now `loadOriginalPictures({ first })` makes the emblems and the house on the screen's Mentat
  first (the campaign passes `this.state.house`) and gives them to the screens; `loadOriginalPicturesWithin` waits
  only for those. The other two Mentats follow one at a time when the browser is idle (`requestIdleCallback`, a
  timer where there is none), and inside a Mentat the page is let through between steps (`breathe()`:
  `scheduler.yield()` or a timer). The colour lookup is also faster (one lookup for each run of a colour). On the
  campaign's house screen, 4x throttled: the screen's pictures in 0.46–0.47 s, all of them in 1.2–1.3 s, the
  longest block 145–149 ms (was 1.4–1.7 s and 283–360 ms); unthrottled 0.18–0.21 s, longest block 57 ms. Not
  fixed: on the main menu (its WebGL backdrop runs, with blocks of 60–120 ms of its own at 4x) blocks still reach
  200–290 ms, mostly the browser's own deflate of the 8x head (CompressionStream runs on the main thread). A
  Worker or a 4x picture would take that away; neither was cheap enough for a minor finding. Without `first`
  (the store reading new files) everything is made together and given when all is made, as before. Test: the
  house on the screen comes first, the others after (tests/original-pictures.test.mjs).
- **The frames were exact only inside their box, though the notes said "edges included".** The real PAKs showed
  52 px (8x) outside the box for Radnor's eyes 3 and 4, 21 for Ammon's eyes 3, 4 for Cyril's book. Now
  `enlargeFigure().part(q, { pad })` returns `{ values, box }`; the eyes and the book are cut REACH (2) pixels
  wider, so with the real PAKs every eye and book frame is exact everywhere (0 wrong pixels inside and outside),
  and the mouth is still cut to the rig's box (16 px outside for three frames, as written above). The rig's
  `lids.box` is the shut eyes' wider box; `lids.left.box` and `lids.right.box`, each eye's mask, are the frame's
  halves as before. The padding adds no pixel inside the mouth box (Radnor's eye frame already reaches 8 rows into
  it; the face hides the CSS eyes while it runs). Tests: tests/pixel-scale.test.mjs checks the whole picture with
  padding, inside the box without, and that without padding something outside differs (the test sees it).
  Checked on the GPU, all three houses at 1920x1080, 1600x900, 1366x768 and 390x844 while he speaks: `fill` 1.000
  everywhere, the face running, no broken picture, no console errors; Radnor's shut eyes shown over the head at
  1920 show no seam (`left-rf-speak-<size>-<house>-<n>.png`, `left-rf-eyes4-crop.png`).
- **The shared-sight test did not test the fog.** It looked at a Sardaukar silo, which `structureVisibleTo` shows
  any ally without asking the fog. Now a player trike and silo beside the Sardaukar base, in both fog and shroud:
  with On the Harkonnen and the Ordos see the ground, the trike and the silo; with Off they do not. With fog
  sharing switched off by hand, the test fails.
- **When the allied computers won, only one was marked the winner.** `endStats` now marks every house still standing
  that is allied with the winner (`friendly()`), so with On all the computers left are winners; Off, nobody has won
  and the rest "fight on", as before. Test: tests/skirmish-allies.test.mjs (fails without the fix).

## For the README

- Skirmish set-up: **Computers allied** (Off by default): On, the computer houses fight as one side against you —
  they never fight each other, share what they see and all come for you; you win when every one of them is out.
  Battle URL: `allied=1`.
- On a phone (620 px and below) the house selection shows each house's bare shield instead of the framed crest.
- With the player's own Dune II files, the original Mentat fills his portrait at any window size: his picture is
  enlarged once by a pixel-art scaler (Scale2x) and drawn smoothly from that, instead of at whole multiples only.
