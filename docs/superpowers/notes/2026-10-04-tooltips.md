# Build tooltips in the style of Command & Conquer 3

Branch `phase3/tooltips` (from `578beeb`). The manager asked (Roman Urdu, translated): "Can you add C&C 3-like
tooltips and tooltip details — a building's power consumption, units' weaknesses and strengths? The build time
shows already; add more like that." And: "Do units build faster with more production buildings, barracks,
factories?" — yes: each extra factory of a line adds 25 % up to twice the speed (`sim/production.js` `lineSpeed`),
and the tooltips and the selection panel now say so where it applies.

## What it does

Every build icon — structures, units, factory upgrades, Starport wares, the Palace weapon — opens a card left of
the sidebar after a 260 ms rest (at once when the pointer moves on from an open card, and on keyboard focus):

- **Header** in the house colour: the icon, the name, a one-line role.
- **State line**: "Click to build · Shift + click for five", "Building 45 % · 12 s left · 2 more on order",
  "Waiting for credits at 30 %", "On hold at 60 % — click resumes, right click cancels", "Waits for the
  Construction Yard", "Ready — click to place", a ware's stock and Frigate, the Palace's charge.
- **Price and time**: what this house pays now (`itemCost`: the Sega slab and ladder prices in a campaign; a
  ware's price today against its list price) and the build time at the line's speed now, with why: "2 Heavy
  Factories: 25 % faster", "5 Heavy Factories: 100 % faster (the most)", "Another Heavy Factory builds 25 %
  faster", "Low power: 49 % speed" (red). Red credits when the house cannot pay it all yet.
- **Structures**: power made (+100, green) or used (−30, with "then 95 of 100 in use", red with "— low power"
  when it would overload the base), storage, hit points, size; what the building **builds** (with the factory
  level each unit still waits for: "Quad · level 1") and what it **leads to** (with what else each still needs:
  "Hi-Tech Factory · Radar Outpost"); abilities (extra factories build faster, comes with a free Harvester,
  infantry can capture it below 25 %, one per house, half rate of fire on low power …).
- **Units**: hit points, class (Infantry, Wheeled, Tracked, Aircraft), sight, speed on sand and on rock; the
  weapon (damage, range, reload; tags: two shots above half health, Anti-air, Always hits / Scatters, Fires on
  the move); **Strong vs** and **Weak vs** (below); abilities (crushes infantry, captures buildings, deploys,
  self-destructs, turns units for 40 s, the wave hurts own units too, flies, airlifts, gathers 700 a load, explodes
  when destroyed …). Unarmed units say so.
- **Upgrades**: "All your Heavy Factories to level 2 of 4", what the level opens and what those still need
  ("Ornithopter · House of IX"), and that it starts at once.
- **Needs** (red, with a lock): what the house still lacks for an item, by name and level ("Heavy Factory level
  4"). The strip lists only what can be built, so on the strip this shows inside the Builds / Leads to / opens
  lists; `missingFor` answers it for any item.
- **Palace weapon**: what it does (17 blasts of up to 150; five Fremen squads within eight tiles, with the Fremen's
  strong and weak; the Saboteur's 500 + 300) and its recharge (the Sega pace in a campaign).

The **selection panel** gains compact facts under the details: a unit's or turret's weapon ("Cannon 25 dmg · range
4 · reload 2.0 s"), Strong and Weak, and an own factory's build speed ("Build speed 125 % · 2 on this line", "100 %
· another one adds 25 %"). Enemy units show theirs (a type is no secret); enemy buildings stay without intel.

## Strong and weak: fought out, not written

`src/data/effectiveness.js` holds a table of real battles. `tests/effectiveness-fights.test.mjs` plays every pair of
fighting types (13 ground units, the Ornithopter, the Gun and Rocket Turret: 119 pairs) in the game's own World:

- equal money at two budgets (about 1000 and 2000 credits: 1 Deviator against 12 Light Infantry, 4 Combat Tanks
  against 12 Infantry Squads …), seeds 1–4, on rock and on sand, each house on either flank — 16 battles a pair;
- the two groups attack-move at each other; whoever stands idle (arrived, or back from Deviator gas) is sent to just
  inside its range of the nearest enemy; turrets stand, powered by Wind Traps, while the units come at them;
- a battle ends when one side is dead or after four minutes; the score is the share of hit points the winner still
  has, counting only units their own house holds at the end (a gassed unit is not its side's until it comes back):
  +1 a walkover, 0 an even trade, −1 a rout.

So everything the game does is in it: overkill, scatter, range and speed, the Sonic wave hitting several units at
once, gassed units fighting their friends, death blasts inside a packed group, tanks running soldiers down when
their path crosses them. `UPDATE_EFFECTIVENESS=1 node --test tests/effectiveness-fights.test.mjs` plays the table
again (worker threads, about a minute) and writes it into the file; the plain test run fails with that command
when the units', weapons' or combat tuning's numbers no longer match the ones the table was fought with
(`FOUGHT_WITH`, a hash), and fights six of the tooltips' claims again.

Classes: Infantry, Light vehicles, Tanks, Aircraft, Turrets (the only buildings that shoot back). A class averaging
≥ 0.3 is "strong", ≤ −0.3 "weak"; single types at ±0.6 are named (up to three a side) when their class is not
listed that way — the Rocket Turret is strong against tanks and weak against the Missile Tank. Looked up per type
and cached.

What the battles say:

| Unit | Strong vs | Weak vs |
|---|---|---|
| Light Infantry | — | Infantry, Light vehicles, Tanks, Aircraft, Turrets |
| Infantry Squad | — | Light vehicles, Tanks, Aircraft, Turrets, Trooper Squad |
| Heavy Trooper | Aircraft, Light Infantry | Light vehicles, Tanks, Turrets |
| Trooper Squad / Fremen | Infantry, Aircraft | Light vehicles, Tanks, Turrets |
| Trike | Infantry | Aircraft, Turrets, Deviator |
| Raider Trike | Infantry | Light vehicles, Tanks, Aircraft, Turrets |
| Quad | Infantry | Aircraft, Turrets, Deviator |
| Combat Tank | Infantry, Light vehicles | Aircraft, Turrets |
| Siege Tank | Infantry | Aircraft, Turrets |
| Missile Tank | Infantry, Tanks, Aircraft, Turrets | — |
| Sonic Tank | Infantry, Gun Turret | Aircraft, Rocket Turret |
| Devastator | Infantry | Tanks, Aircraft, Turrets |
| Deviator | Infantry, Light vehicles | Aircraft, Turrets |

Close calls the lists leave out (between −0.3 and 0.3): the light vehicles against tanks (the Trike −0.24, the Quad
−0.06 on average), the Siege Tank against light vehicles (0.12) and tanks (0.02), the Combat Tank against tanks
(0.20), the Deviator against tanks (0.06). This is the remake's balance as it plays; the tooltips report it and make
no case for changing it.

## Look and feel (ui-ux-pro-max)

`search.py "RTS game HUD tooltip dark translucent military" --design-system`: pattern Immersive/Interactive, style
**HUD / Sci-Fi FUI** (fine 1 px lines, decorative corner markers, transparency, monospaced data, contrast 4.5:1,
keyboard and visible focus, reduced motion); typography Share Tech Mono / Fira Code (style domain: HUD and
Cyberpunk UI; typography domain: Barlow Condensed). UX domain: Color Only (icons and words besides colour), Focus
Not Obscured, Hover vs Tap, Hover States.

Used: dark translucent card (rgba 13,10,7,.92), 1 px lines, corner ticks, monospaced numbers, a header in the
house colour (`--house`, set by the game view), ▲ Strong / ▼ Weak / lock + "Needs" (never colour alone), 12 px
body text and 11 px small caps labels, fade and 6 px slide in 120 ms, none under `prefers-reduced-motion`,
`:focus-visible` ring on the icons, `role="tooltip"` and `aria-describedby`, Esc dismisses. Not used: the neon
cyan palette (the game's sand-and-gold frame and the house colours instead), web fonts (the game ships its own
Trebuchet / Consolas stack, no network), backdrop blur (a GPU cost over the WebGL canvas every frame).

Placement: left of the sidebar, level with the icon, a notch in the house colour on the icon's middle; pushed up
or down to stay 8 px inside the window (1366x768 and 1920x1080 checked); fixed width, the state line on one line,
so nothing jumps as it changes; it follows the icon when the strip scrolls and carries on when an upgrade icon is
rebuilt at its next level.

Iterations on the real GPU (`?scene=skirmish&house=atreides&credits=5000&deploy=1`, `?scene=base&house=harkonnen`,
`?scene=mission&house=atreides&mission=4`, at 1920x1080 and 1366x768; shots kept out of the repo): v1 two-column stats and "9  s" in monospace;
v2 three-column stats, speed on its own row, "sec build time", Strong/Weak labels on one line, factory lists by
level; v3 the frame in the house colour with a faint glow, shorter upgrade role, the state span kept for good
(scripts read `.sb-tip span`); v4 wording.

## Performance

The card is built only when it opens (`sidebarModel().describe(item)`, one function per world and house); each
frame after that compares a few plain fields of the icon's item and touches the page only when the state line
changes; the card is rebuilt when the price or the power changes and once a second while open (factories built,
prices re-rolled). Nothing is built while no card is open. Static facts per type, each type's Strong / Weak (a lookup in the battle
table) and the panel's compact facts are cached (the panel's lists frozen).

## Review fixes

- **Strong / Weak contradicted the game** (critical). The paper model left out what decides many fights — the
  Sonic wave hitting about two Trikes a shot, Deviator gas turning about two soldiers a burst who then fight their
  friends, the death blasts of Trikes, Raiders and Missile Tanks inside their own group — and added a crush term
  that tanks on attack-move rarely earn. It is gone: the table is now the outcome of real World battles (above),
  written by `tests/effectiveness-fights.test.mjs`. Checked against the review's own harness (`sweep.mjs`: 78 ground
  pairs, another arena, four runs each): 2 pairs of 78 disagree, down from 19, and neither changes a class verdict.
  The Deviator is now strong against infantry and light vehicles; Raiders are weak against tanks; Trikes and Quads
  no longer claim tanks; the heavy tanks no longer claim weakness to light vehicles.
- **False matchups in the report and notes** (important). The "cheap buggies beat expensive tanks" paragraph and
  its rebalance suggestion are removed; the table, the close calls and the README paragraph follow the battles.
- **Tab to a hidden strip icon broke the arrows** (important, older than this branch). Focus made the browser
  scroll `.sb-slots` itself (scrollTop 928) while the strip's offset stayed 0, so the arrows could never bring the
  first icon back. Now focus scrolls the strip by its offset to just show the icon (`reveal`), and the box's own
  scroll is held at 0. Real GPU, base scene 1366x768: focusing the Carryall gives translateY(-1012px), scrollTop 0;
  twelve up-arrow clicks bring the first icon back to the top of the strip. `tests/sidebar-strip.test.mjs`.
- **Tests checked the model against itself** (minor). `effectiveness-fights` fights six published claims again in
  real battles (Deviator vs Light Infantry, Siege Tank vs Trikes, Combat Tanks vs Infantry Squads, Missile Tanks vs
  Gun Turrets, Raiders vs Combat Tanks, Ornithopters vs Trooper Squads) and fails when the table is stale.
- **First hover hitch** (minor). No scatter integrals are left at hover time: a type's Strong / Weak is a lookup
  in the table, all 16 types together in about 5 ms cold, then cached.
