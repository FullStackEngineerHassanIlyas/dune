# Audio fixes: one victory fanfare, the Announcer option mid-battle (2026-10-06)

Branch fix/fanfare-announcer from master c1a558c.

## 1. The victory fanfare started again on the results

**What was wrong.** After a won campaign mission the Carryall fly-over started the house's victory theme in the
battle's frame (24bcb7b). The frame then closed, `MenuMusic.enter()` started the title on the menu's music, and
the results' `mood('victory:<house>')` started the fanfare again from the top. Reproduced in Chrome on the GPU,
base commit, with a real click and no autoplay flag: the battle played `victory-atreides` over the Carryalls, then
the menu's music logged `[title, victory-atreides]` 2 ms apart as the results opened.

**Fix.** The fanfare now plays on the menu's music from the start, so there is only one.
- `GameView.fanfare()` (src/game/game-view.js): in the menu shell the fly-over posts `{ dune: 'fanfare', house,
  held }` and the battle's own music stays silent ('over'). With no shell the battle plays it itself, as before.
  `holdFanfare()` posts `held` again when P or M changes during the fly-over, once per change. `showMissionEnd()`
  (the shell did not take the result in 5 s) does not start a second fanfare.
- `MenuMusic.fanfare(house, { held })` (src/audio/music/music.js) wakes the menu's audio behind the frame and
  plays `victory:<house>`. While `held` it rests: the context is suspended and a player's file is paused, as on a
  hidden page. `enter({ carry: true })` keeps the fanfare playing instead of restarting the title.
- src/scenes/menu.js: the 'fanfare' handler reads the saved settings first, because the battle's Options may have
  changed the volume. `quit(screen)` passes `carry: screen === 'campaign-results'`. Any other screen, such as the
  hub after a result the campaign could not read, gets the title from its start, so the fanfare never loops under
  it.
- The campaign screens needed no change. Their victory card, Mentat and score already ask for `victory:<house>`,
  and the music already playing carries on. The password screen starts the Mentat's theme, as before.

**Proof.**
- tests/final-audio-flyover.test.mjs has three new tests. "in the menu shell the fanfare starts once ..." and
  "rests while the battle is paused or muted" failed on c1a558c (no 'fanfare' was posted, and the menu's context
  stayed suspended). "back from a battle with no fanfare to carry on ..." covers the title coming back. The two
  existing tests, including the no-shell case, still pass.
- Chrome on the GPU (`--use-angle=gl`, a real click on the region's Start now, no autoplay flag), Atreides
  mission 1 won:
  - The menu's music logged one start, `[[9034, "victory-atreides"]]`, at the fly-over and nothing after it.
  - It sounded at about -28 dB RMS over the Carryalls, -27 on the victory card, -37 under the Mentat's words
    (ducked) and -28 on the score.
  - The battle's own music stayed at `over`. There were no console errors.
- The same check with P, P, M, M during the fly-over: the context went suspended, running, suspended, running,
  and there was still only one start. With Esc during the fly-over (straight to the results) there was one start
  too.
- Alone (`?scene=mission`) the mood goes `over` → `victory:atreides` at the fly-over and stays there on the end
  screen. The music cannot sound headless without a gesture, so the play count comes from the unit test.

**Checked and left alone.**
- Returning from a battle still sends `play title` just before a defeat or victory mood. The mixer drops a track
  that has not sounded yet (`fadeAll`: `pos > 0`), so nothing is heard.
- The menu's context resumes without a new gesture because the page already has sticky activation from the
  player's clicks on the menu. The GPU run above had no autoplay flag.

## 2. Options → Announcer only applied from the next battle

**What was wrong.** `GameView.applySetting` had no 'announcer' case. `WebVoiceOutput.setAnnouncer` already existed
and had tests, but nothing called it. Reproduced in Chrome: clicking "Each house" in the game menu left
`__dune.voice()` at `{ announcer: 'one', set: 'announcer' }`.

**Fix.** `applySetting('announcer', v)` calls `announcer.player.output.setAnnouncer(v)`. Only the lines whose voice
changed are let go, and the commonest are decoded again at once (voice.js).

**Proof.**
- tests/announcer-shared.test.mjs "Options → Announcer in the game menu applies at once ..." failed on c1a558c
  (`'one'` instead of `'house'`). It switches both ways and checks that the next line is fetched from the
  voice chosen.
- In Chrome, Options → "Each house" mid-battle gives `{ announcer: 'house', set: 'atreides' }`, and
  "One voice" gives `{ announcer: 'one', set: 'announcer' }` again.

## Review fixes

- **The shell's side of the fanfare had no test.** The tests copied menu.js's routing by hand, so dropping the
  'fanfare' handler or mistyping the `carry` check still passed `node --test`. The routing, the `quit()` music and
  settings step, and the 'fanfare' handler moved out of `start()` into `battleShell({ music, settings, reload,
  close, show })`, exported from src/scenes/menu.js. `start()` passes in the page parts (closing the frame, the
  backdrop, `menu.show`). tests/final-audio-flyover.test.mjs now sends the battle's messages through it, with a
  campaign stand-in that, like ui/campaign/index.js, calls `shell.quit('campaign-results')` on 'missionEnd'. A
  new test covers the `house` guard and the settings reload. Five mutations of `battleShell` (no 'fanfare' entry,
  a mistyped `carry` screen, no guard, no reload in `fanfare`, no reload in `quit`) each fail 1 to 4 of its tests.
- **The `original` symlink was not ignored.** `original/` in .gitignore matches only a folder, and
  `git check-ignore original` printed nothing. Added `/original`, which also matches a symlink.
