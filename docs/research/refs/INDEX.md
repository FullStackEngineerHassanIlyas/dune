# Dune II / Dune: The Battle for Arrakis — Visual Reference Index

Private design-reference images only (not for shipping). Originally 47 files, ~1.5 MB; with the
structure-research additions at the end of this file, about 100 files, ~5.4 MB (September 2026).
All files verified with `file` to be genuine JPEG/GIF/PNG (no HTML/error pages).

Note on sourcing: **The Spriters Resource** (spriters-resource.com) and **dune.fandom.com**,
**MobyGames**, **GameFAQs**, **Sega Retro**, **TCRF**, **ModDB** and the **Dune RTS wiki**
(dunerts.wiki.gg) all sit behind Cloudflare/Anubis bot-challenges that block both `curl` and
the fetch tool — none of their sprite sheets could be retrieved despite being the intended
primary source. Substitutes below cover the same ground: **dune2k.com** (long-running Dune II
fan site) has per-unit/structure in-game info-card screenshots for the PC version; **Internet
Archive**'s emulated software items provided real auto-captured PC and Genesis screenshots;
**retro-replay.com** and **regvault.org** filled in Genesis gameplay screens and box art.

## Notes / gaps

- **Genesis and PC sprite sheets (multi-frame, all rotations)** could not be retrieved: The
  Spriters Resource blocks all automated access (Cloudflare "Just a moment" challenge on every
  page, including direct sheet URLs). The per-unit/structure "info card" screenshots above are
  the best available substitute — each shows clean official artwork of the subject, just not
  every rotation frame.
- **Command & Conquer (1995) sidebar screenshot** (item 8) could not be obtained: MobyGames,
  cnc.fandom.com and ModDB are all bot-blocked, Wikipedia's C&C article carries only cover art
  under fair use, and Wikimedia Commons was unreachable from this environment. Not included —
  box art alone wouldn't serve the "sidebar interface" reference purpose that was asked for.
- `genesis-screenshot-mentat-atreides-map.png` is from a prototype cartridge dump (archive.org
  identifier `dune_20221005`), not the final retail ROM — visually very close to retail but
  flagged in case that matters for reference accuracy.

  (blue power bar when selected, low-power lightning icon drawn over it), and the navy building with two white-rimmed
